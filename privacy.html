Privacy Policy

Learn how this website handles information during its practice activities.

1. About This Website

CareBridge Healthcare is currently being used as a website practice project to learn website functionality, analytics and conversion tracking. This site should not be treated as a substitute for professional medical advice or a verified healthcare provider.

2. Forms and Information

The appointment and contact forms are practice forms. They demonstrate browser validation and page navigation. They do not send submissions to a provider or save them to a website database.

3. Analytics and Tracking

This website may use Google Tag Manager and, after setup, Google Analytics to help its owner learn about website interactions. Analytics configuration must be reviewed before public use.

4. Sensitive Information

Do not enter medical details, health conditions, passwords, payment information or other sensitive personal information into practice forms. Such information must not be sent to analytics tools.

5. Third-Party Services

Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics or other services may process technical information when enabled. Their applicable policies and settings should be reviewed before using this website publicly.

6. Contact

This is a practice project and no verified healthcare contact has been configured yet. Do not use this page to submit urgent medical enquiries.

This is a sample privacy notice for learning purposes, not a legal review or a final policy for a real healthcare business.
